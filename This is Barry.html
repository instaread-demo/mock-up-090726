Fall Movie Ending Explained: 5 Key Twists and Questions Answered

Fall is a 2022 survival thriller directed by Scott Mann, starring Grace Caroline Currey as Becky and Virginia Gardner as Hunter. Two best friends climb a 2,000-foot TV tower, get stranded at the top, and everything goes wrong. If you’ve just watched it and need a straight-talking Fall movie ending explained guide, you’re in the right place. Here’s the plot and ending of Fall explained; spoilers ahead.

Long Story Short

  • Becky’s husband Dan died in a climbing accident, and she’s been a recluse for a year.
  • Hunter suggests climbing a decommissioned 2,000-foot B-67 TV tower to scatter Dan’s ashes.
  • The ladder breaks, stranding them on a tiny platform with no phone signal, food, or water.
  • They try to send a distress message by dropping Hunter’s phone in a shoe, but it shatters uselessly below.
  • Hunter descends on a short rope to retrieve a backpack stuck on a satellite dish but slips and falls to her death.
  • Becky, delirious, imagines Hunter is still alive and helping her. The twist: Hunter died days ago.
  • Becky eventually charges the drone using a light, sends a final message for help, and is rescued after about a week.

Fall: Plot Explained

The film opens with Becky, her husband Dan, and best friend Hunter climbing a mountain. A bat startles Dan, he loses his grip, and he falls to his death. One year later, Becky still hasn’t moved on. She’s avoiding her father James, who thinks she should be stronger, and she’s even considered suicide. Hunter shows up just before the anniversary and proposes climbing the B-67 TV tower, an old, rusted structure in the desert, to scatter Dan’s ashes from the top and help Becky heal. Reluctantly, Becky agrees.

The next day, they drive out and start climbing the severely corroded ladder. They reach the tiny platform at the top, where Hunter goads Becky into hanging off the edge while she records for her YouTube channel. Becky scatters the ashes, but as they begin their descent, the ladder breaks. Becky falls just a little before her safety rope catches her. They are now trapped with no way down, no phone signal, and limited supplies.

They try a desperate plan: drop Hunter’s phone in one of their shoes with a message, hoping it catches a signal at the base. It smashes to pieces with no help coming. Hunger and thirst set in. Vultures circle. Hunter later reveals a shocking truth: she cheated with Dan before he died, as proven by a “1-4-3” tattoo on her ankle (meaning “I love you”) that matches one Dan had. Becky is furious, but they have no choice but to work together.

Hunter volunteers to go down the little rope they have to retrieve a backpack Becky accidentally dropped onto a satellite dish. She unhooks her safety line, jumps, and grabs the dish, but as she tries to hook the bag to the rope, she loses her footing and falls to her death. Becky, already delirious, imagines that Hunter made it back up and is still helping her. She hallucinates entire conversations and a plan to charge the drone using the tower’s signal light to send a message. In reality, she’s alone the whole time.

Fall Movie Ending Explained

This is the part where we answer the big question: what actually happens in the Fall movie ending? Let’s break it down step by step so it’s clear.

The twist: Hunter was dead all along

The key reveal is that Hunter died when she fell from the satellite dish. Becky, suffering from severe dehydration and hunger, conjures an imaginary Hunter to keep herself going. The audience sees this fake Hunter for a large chunk of the final act, but the moment Becky realises the truth is a gut punch. She finds Hunter’s body far below, still holding the backpack. The phone in the shoe was destroyed. The drone’s first failed message never happened. Everything after the fall until that point was a hallucination.

How Becky finally gets rescued

Becky, now alone and on the edge of giving up, gets attacked by a vulture. She fights it off, kills it, and eats it to regain some strength. Then, using the tower’s signal light, she charges the drone’s battery enough to send a single message to her father James: a photo of herself with a location pin. The message doesn’t explain everything, but it’s enough for him to alert emergency services. After about a week on the tower, Becky is finally pulled to safety.

What Does the “1-4-3” Tattoo Mean?

Early in the film, we see a tattoo on Hunter’s ankle: “1-4-3”. It stands for “I love you” (one letter, four letters, three letters). Later, Hunter confesses that she and Dan had matching tattoos because they were having an affair. This adds a whole layer of betrayal to the already tense situation and explains why Becky is so conflicted about trusting her friend. I personally feel this twist works well because it comes out of nowhere but makes sense of earlier awkward moments.

How Long Was Becky Trapped?

The film doesn’t give an exact number of days, but it’s easy to estimate. Becky is on the tower long enough to become delirious from lack of food and water, and to lose track of what’s real. After the drone is destroyed (or so she thinks), she spends several more days before the vulture attack. By the time she’s rescued, it’s likely been about a week. The Wikipedia page for Fall corroborates the film’s events and timeline.

How Did Becky Survive?

Becky’s survival comes down to a few key moments. First, after realising Hunter is dead, she uses the drone, but its battery dies mid-flight. She then connects it to the tower’s signal light and waits for it to charge. That gives her enough power to send a final message with her location to her father. Just before that, she kills and eats a vulture that attacks her, which gives her the physical strength to keep going. It’s a rough, messy survival, but it all pays off in the end.

Is Fall Based on a True Story?

No, Fall is entirely fictional. There isn’t a real decommissioned B-67 tower, and the characters aren’t real people. However, the film taps into a very real fear: being stuck somewhere high with no escape. The filmmakers shot on an actual high tower to get realistic vertigo effects, which makes it feel terrifyingly authentic. My Take on the Film

Honestly, I think Fall is a solid survival thriller that does exactly what it sets out to do: make your palms sweat and your stomach churn. The twist is well-crafted, and Grace Caroline Currey carries the emotional weight of a woman grieving, betrayed, and fighting for her life. The vulture scene is a bit absurd, but it works in a desperate, primal kind of way. The biggest weakness is perhaps the logic of the drone and phone, but suspending your disbelief is part of the deal with these movies. It’s not a flawless film, but it’s a memorable one, and the twist alone makes it worth discussing.

Frequently asked questions

What is the twist ending of Fall?

The twist reveals that Hunter actually died earlier while trying to retrieve the backpack. Becky was hallucinating Hunter’s presence for several days, and the rescue mission she thought Hunter was carrying out was just in her mind.

How did Hunter die in Fall?

Hunter fell to her death after unhooking her safety rope and jumping to a satellite dish to get the backpack. She lost her grip and plunged from the tower.

What does the 143 tattoo mean in Fall?

The 1-4-3 tattoo stands for ‘I love you’ (1 letter, 4 letters, 3 letters). It reveals that Hunter had an affair with Becky’s late husband Dan, as he had a matching tattoo.

How long was Becky trapped on the tower in Fall?

Becky was stranded for about a week. She was up there long enough to become delirious from hunger and dehydration, and to eventually kill and eat a vulture for strength.
